# Data Sources, Assumptions and Limitations

## Primary dataset (Feature 3 input)

- Dataset: SolarSphere_Feature3_Demo_Dataset_Location_Based.xlsx, sheet "Feature 1 Outputs".
- Source: outputs of Feature 1 (AI Rooftop Solar Potential Analysis) generated by the project
  team for demonstration.
- Fields used: Location_Input, Rooftop_Selection, Latitude, Longitude, Locality,
  Rooftop_Area_m2, Usable_Rooftop_Area_m2, Recommended_Panels, System_Capacity_kW,
  Annual_Generation_kWh, Estimated_Installation_Cost_INR, Estimated_Annual_Savings_INR,
  Payback_Period_Years, Suitability, Data_Type.
- Access date: 2026-09-27.
- Classification: D - synthetic demonstration data. Every row carries Data_Type =
  SYNTHETIC_DEMO. It must not be presented as a real-world measurement. It can later be
  replaced by real Feature 1 outputs without changing the Feature 3 architecture.

## Basemap (map tiles)

- Source: OpenStreetMap raster tiles via the standard Leaflet tile layer.
- Purpose: geographic visualization of rooftop points and locality centroids.
- Classification: C - real public data.
- License: OpenStreetMap data is licensed ODbL; tiles are served under the OpenStreetMap
  tile usage policy.

## Emission factor for CO2 reduction

- Default: 0.71 kg CO2 per kWh.
- Basis: indicative weighted grid emission factor for the Indian power sector published in
  the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO2 Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector.
- Classification: E - user-configured assumption. The value is editable in the dashboard and
  in the report; the applied value and its label are always displayed with the result.

## Assumptions made by Feature 3

1. One Excel row equals one analyzed rooftop/terrace from Feature 1.
2. Locality is taken from the Locality column; it is never randomly assigned.
3. All monetary values are INR as estimated by Feature 1.
4. Annual generation, cost, savings and payback are used exactly as produced by Feature 1;
   Feature 3 does not re-derive them.
5. The Solar Potential Index is a project decision-support score, not an official government
   rating.
6. Budget scenarios are simulations and confer no approval or entitlement.
7. Index weights are configurable and default to 25/25/20/15/10/5.

## Limitations

- Demonstration rows are synthetic, so absolute magnitudes are illustrative only.
- No shading, structural load, roof orientation, or grid-connection studies are performed by
  Feature 3; those belong to Feature 1 or to detailed engineering.
- Localities are administrative groupings as given in the dataset; no legal boundary
  validation is performed.
- Emission factor is a national average and does not represent a specific state grid or a
  specific hour of generation.
